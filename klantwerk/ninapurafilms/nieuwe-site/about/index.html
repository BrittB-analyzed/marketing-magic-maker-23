About

Hi, I’m Nina

A Dutch photographer and videographer, living in Ibiza. Storytelling has always been the common thread in my life.

After years in corporate sales I realised I was putting the sensitive, creative part of myself aside to hit targets. Picking up my camera again gave me back the energy I had missed. Today, sensitivity and empathy are exactly what I use in my work: to make people feel at ease and to capture who they really are.

I work with three themes – love, life and purity – and I don’t believe in one size fits all. Before every shoot we talk personally, and I do all filming and editing myself.
