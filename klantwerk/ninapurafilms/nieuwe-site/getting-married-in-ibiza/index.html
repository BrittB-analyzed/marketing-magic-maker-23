Getting married in Ibiza

Everything you need to know before you start planning: where, when, what it costs and how to make it legal.

Can you legally get married in Ibiza?

A civil wedding in Spain usually requires at least one partner to be a legal resident, which makes it complicated for most international couples. The common solution: make it legal at home and celebrate with a symbolic ceremony in Ibiza. A Catholic church wedding is possible but requires extra paperwork through your home parish.

The best time to get married in Ibiza

May, June and September are the sweet spot: warm, golden light and fewer crowds. July and August are the hottest and busiest months with the highest venue rates. October is still warm and often quieter.

Golden hour is a photographer’s favourite: soft, warm light that flatters everyone. In summer that means a ceremony around 18:00 and portraits around sunset.

What does a wedding in Ibiza cost?

It depends mostly on the venue, guest count and date. Venue hire, catering and accommodation make up the largest share; photography and film are typically a smaller but lasting part of the budget. Get quotes early for peak-season Saturdays.

Budget itemWhat to consider

Frequently asked questions

What time is sunset in Ibiza in summer?

In June and July the sun sets after 21:00, which makes a late-afternoon ceremony followed by sunset portraits a popular choice.
