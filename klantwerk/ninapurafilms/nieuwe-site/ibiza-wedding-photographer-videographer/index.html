FAQ

Questions about your Ibiza wedding

Do you offer both photography and film?

Yes. I can capture your wedding in photos, on film, or both. Booking photo and film with one person means one calm presence on your day instead of two crews, and one consistent style across your photos and your film.

Where in Ibiza do you work?

All over the island: fincas in the countryside, cliffside venues on the west coast with views of Es Vedrà, beach clubs, private villas and the old town of Dalt Vila. I also travel to Formentera and worldwide.

Do you use a drone?

Yes, when the location and regulations allow it. Parts of Ibiza – such as the airport zone and protected natural areas – have restrictions, so I check permissions per venue in advance.

How far in advance should we book?

Peak season dates (May to October, especially Saturdays) often book 9–15 months ahead. Smaller elopements and proposals can sometimes be arranged within a few weeks.
